# Send Feedback and Report a Bug

## Overview

Use **Send feedback** to tell the Quizzer team about bugs, confusing UI,
performance issues, or feature ideas. Submissions go to the Quizzer
support inbox.

Users may call this: report a bug, send feedback, contact support,
suggestion, feature request, something is broken, tell the team.

## How to Open Feedback (current UI)

Do **not** look for Feedback inside the avatar menu. Feedback is a
normal sidebar link.

Desktop (left sidebar):
1. In the left sidebar, scroll to the **ACCOUNT** section (below
   Workspace).
2. Click **Send feedback** (paper-plane / heart-message icon).
3. You open the `/feedback` page titled **Send feedback**.

Mobile:
1. Open the account / more menu from the bottom navigation.
2. Tap **Send feedback**.

Also available: Ask QUE, or open Help & Support and use the link back
from feedback — but the primary path is the sidebar ACCOUNT item.

## Step-by-Step: Report a Bug

1. Click **Send feedback** in the sidebar ACCOUNT section.
2. Under **Your review**, choose category **Bug report** (or tap the
   quick pill **Found a bug**).
3. In the message box, describe: what you expected, what happened,
   and the steps to reproduce. Include the page you were on if you can
   (for example Help, Exams, Monitoring).
4. Optionally leave a star rating and confirm your contact email.
5. Click **Send** / submit. You should see a thank-you confirmation.

Message length is capped (about 1000 characters). Keep steps short and
specific.

## Other Feedback Categories

On the same page you can also choose:
- UI / Design
- Ecosystem
- Performance
- General

Quick pills can prefill the message: Love it, Needs work, Confusing UI,
Found a bug, Feature idea.

## What the Avatar Menu Is For Now

The avatar at the bottom of the sidebar only opens a small menu for
profile identity and **Log out**. Settings, Send feedback, Help &
Support, and Upgrade are listed in the ACCOUNT section of the sidebar
— you do not open the avatar to reach them.

## SAY

- Click **Send feedback** in the left sidebar under **ACCOUNT**.
- For bugs, pick category **Bug report** or the **Found a bug** pill.
- Describe steps to reproduce, then submit.

## NEVER

- Never say: click your avatar, then select Feedback.
- Never invent a support ticket ID or claim a human already read it.
- Never send users to an external form unless product knowledge
  documents one (current path is in-app `/feedback`).
