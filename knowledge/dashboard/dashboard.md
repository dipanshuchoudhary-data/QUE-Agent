# Dashboard

## Overview

The Dashboard is the landing page after logging in. It provides an
at-a-glance overview of your exam activity, live exams, key metrics,
and quick actions.

Users may call this: home, main page, landing page, overview, home
screen.

## How to Access

Click Dashboard in the sidebar, or navigate to the root URL after
logging in. Teachers are redirected to the teacher dashboard after
login. Students see a separate student dashboard.

## Step-by-Step UI Guide: Using the Dashboard

Step 1 — Open Dashboard:
In the left sidebar, click Dashboard (it is the first item, with a
home icon). Or just log in — the Dashboard is your landing page.

Step 2 — Read summary metrics:
At the top, you will see metric cards showing your overall exam
activity: total exams created, total attempts received, and recent
activity. Each card may show a trend arrow.

Step 3 — Check live exams:
Look for the Live Exams panel. If any of your exams have students
actively taking them right now, those exams appear here with a pulsing
LIVE badge. Click on an exam in the live panel to jump directly to
its Monitoring tab.

Step 4 — Use quick actions:
The Dashboard shows quick action cards for common tasks. Click the
Create Exam card to start a new exam immediately.

Step 5 — Follow the activation checklist (new accounts):
If you are a new user, you will see an activation checklist guiding
you through first steps: create your first exam, approve questions,
publish, etc. Click each step to navigate to the relevant page. You
can dismiss the checklist once you are familiar with the product.

## Teacher Dashboard

The teacher dashboard shows:

Summary metrics: key numbers about your overall exam activity (total
exams, total attempts, recent activity).

Live exams panel: exams that currently have LIVE (active, in-progress)
attempts. Shows the LIVE badge and quick access to each exam. Uses the
same LIVE definition as the Exams workspace (published, unsubmitted,
timer running, heartbeat active).

Quick actions: one-click access to Create Exam and other common tasks.

Activation checklist: for newer accounts, a guided checklist of
first steps (create your first exam, approve questions, publish, etc.).
Can be dismissed.

The Dashboard is a summary view. For detailed data, navigate to the
specific workspace (Exams for exam management, Analytics for trends,
Students for learner data).

## Student Dashboard

Student accounts see a different, simpler dashboard:

Welcome message with the student's name.
Status pill confirming the student account is active.
Three informational cards:
- How to use teacher exam links to take exams.
- Tips for staying exam-ready (browser permissions, internet
  connection).
- Overview of the protected assessment flow.

The student dashboard does NOT list available exams or show scores.
Students access exams through links shared by teachers, not through
the dashboard.

## What Makes the Dashboard Empty

The teacher dashboard may show an empty or minimal state when:
- No exams have been created yet (the activation checklist guides you)
- No exams have LIVE activity
- The account is brand new

The Dashboard fills up as you create exams, publish them, and students
start taking them.

## Common Questions

What is on the Dashboard?
An overview of your exam activity: summary metrics, live exams,
quick actions, and (for new accounts) an activation checklist.

Why is my Dashboard empty?
If you have not created any exams yet, the Dashboard shows a getting-
started guide. Create your first exam to see activity here.

I am a student. Why do I only see cards, not my exams?
Student accounts have a simplified dashboard that explains how to use
exam links. To take an exam, open a link shared by your teacher.

Where do I see detailed data?
The Dashboard is a summary. For exam management go to Exams, for
performance trends go to Analytics, for learner data go to Students.

What does LIVE mean on the Dashboard?
It means a student has an active, in-progress attempt on that exam
right now, with their browser tab open and timer running.
